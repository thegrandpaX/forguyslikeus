FOR GUYS LIKE US -- POCKET CARD
----------------------------------------------------------------------

IF YOU'RE IN CRISIS
  Call or text 988. Free, confidential, 24/7.
  You don't need the right words. Just call.
  Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
  Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988, then press 1
  SAMHSA Helpline (drugs & alcohol): 1-800-662-4357

WHEN IT'S MORE THAN A ROUGH PATCH
  If several of these stick around for weeks:
  - Irritable, angry, or on edge nearly every day
  - Pain, headaches, or stomach trouble with no clear cause
  - Drinking, smoking, or pills increasing just to get through the day
  - Sleep is wrecked, or 10 hours and still exhausted
  - Detached from family, friends, everything you cared about
  - Taking risks because you don't care what happens
  - Thinking everyone would be better off without you

FIRST STEPS, SIMPLEST FIRST
  1. Get a checkup and bloodwork. Be honest about sleep and drinking.
  2. Breathe: in 4 seconds, hold 4, out slow for 6. Two minutes.
  3. Cut energy drinks after noon. Cut back drinking on weeknights.
  4. Tell one person: "Work and life have been beating the hell
     out of me lately."
  5. Find a counselor who gets it. A bad one gets replaced.
  6. Check your EAP or union member assistance program.
