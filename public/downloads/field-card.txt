FIELD CARD // FOR GUYS LIKE US                          REV. 2026.04
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Keep this laminated in your glove compartment or inside your gangbox lid.

1. EMERGENCY
   DIAL OR TEXT 988 [PEER + CLINICAL] -- free, confidential, 24/7/365.
   Veterans: dial 988, then press 1. Or text 838255.

2. UNION RIGHTS
   CBA medical emergency provisions / FMLA coverage -- up to 12 weeks
   of job-protected leave. Employers get functional clearance only,
   never your therapy notes.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
   Mandated by federal HIPAA (45 CFR Section 164) and the ADA.
   Diagnoses cannot be disclosed to foremen, PMs, or GC dispatch
   without your written consent.

4. THE GAUGE -- WARNING SIGNS
   - Snapping explosively at crew or family over small mistakes
   - Needing alcohol, pills, or weed to quiet the internal alarm
   - Deep apathy about standard job safety rules
   - Waking up already empty, dreading the ignition key turn
   - Believing your family would be better off without you
   2+ checked? Engage support today. Don't wait for the morning shift.
